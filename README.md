**Naga City Dengue Surveillance & Outbreak Control Insights**

 📌**Executive Summary**

This report presents key data insights and action plans based on the Naga City Dengue Dashboard. The goal is to lower dengue cases, protect high-risk age groups, and keep community health systems strong.

📈 **Key Metrics Overview**

* 🦠 Total Dengue Cases: Naga City recorded 1,000 total cases.

* ⚠️ Attack Rate: Standing at 53.30 per 10,000 population, showing a moderate to high infection rate across the city.

* 🏥 Hospitalization Rate: At 36.53%, nearly 4 out of 10 patients needed hospital admission. This places a high load on local medical facilities.

* 🌧️ Average Monthly Rainfall: Averages 133.68 mm, creating ideal breeding conditions for mosquitoes in stagnant water.

🔍 **Key Observations & Data Patterns**
    **🧒Vulnerable Demographic (Age Groups)**

* Most cases occur among Young Adults and Minors, with much lower counts in Adults and Seniors.

* **Reason:** Children and students spend significant time in schools, daycares, and playgrounds during early morning and late afternoon hours when mosquitoes actively bite.

**📍High-Risk Areas & Geographic Clustering**

* The top 3 ranked barangays with the most cases are Concepcion Pequeña, San Felipe, and Cararayan.

* **Reason:** These barangays are densely populated and located near major rivers (salog) and waterways. Heavy rainfall combined with river overflow creates stagnant water in canals and drainage systems, speeding up mosquito breeding.

**🚀Proposed Action Plans & Recommendations**

**💨Action Plan A: Targeted Weekly Fogging**

* Rotational Schedule: Implement fogging in 3 to 4 barangays per week, starting with the highest-risk areas (Concepcion Pequeña, San Felipe, Cararayan, and Pacol).

* Strategic Timing: Perform fogging during peak mosquito activity hours: early morning (5:00 AM – 7:00 AM) and late afternoon (4:00 PM – 6:00 PM).

 **🧹Action Plan B: Community Clean-Up Task Force ("Task Force Kalinigan kang Lugar")**

* Citywide Partnership: The City Mayor and Barangay Captains will form a dedicated sanitation task force.

* Weekly Clean-Up Drives: Require every zone (purok) to hold weekly clean-ups focused on clearing clogged canals, drainage pathways, riverbanks, and open containers that collect rainwater.

**🧴Action Plan C: Barangay Dengue Prevention & Relief Kits**

* Preventive Care Kits: Use local barangay funds to distribute simple relief kits to households, especially in active hotspot zones.

**Kit Contents:**

🧴 Mosquito repellent lotion

💊 Vitamin C / Multivitamins (to boost immunity)

🧼 Antibacterial soap and larvicides (to treat standing water in household storage)
